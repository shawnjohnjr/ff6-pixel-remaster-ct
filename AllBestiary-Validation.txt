機制／Mechanism
Copy the existing defeat dictionary; add minimum missing counts through
PictureBookSaveManager.UpdateDefeatCount; verify completion from
PictureBookClient.GetPictureBooks; save through PictureBookSaveManager.Save;
recalculate through AchievementManager.UpdatePictureBook and upload through
AchievementManager.UploadUserStats. Read back the states using
SteamSDKManager.GetAllAchievementsOnPlatform (backed by Steam GetAchievement).
No direct Steam unlock call or hard-coded achievement progress write is used.

限制／Limits

long-term play were not tested. The verified persistence is a shared-file reload.
Backups stay local and are not included in this public repository.
